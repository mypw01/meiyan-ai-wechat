About MEIYAN ENVIRO TECH

Dongguan Meiyan Environmental Technology Co., Ltd. manufactures high-pressure misting equipment in Dongguan, Guangdong, China. Pump units, ruby-orifice nozzles, control panels and cabinets are produced and pressure tested in our own workshop, so systems are configured to the site instead of picked from a fixed range. Applications: landscape fog, outdoor cooling, outdoor mosquito control, dust suppression for construction and bulk material sites, odor control for waste facilities, and humidification for poultry sheds and greenhouses.

How it works
Why the effect is stable and repeatable
🍃

High-pressure fine mist

A plunger pump holds 70–100 bar and forces water through stainless nozzles as 1–15 μm droplets. The mist evaporates fast, so it cools the air without soaking people or furniture.

🟞

Perimeter mosquito control

Nozzles around the boundary are fed by the same pump platform at 40–70 bar with 15–40 μm droplets. Scheduled cycles treat the area at dawn and dusk, before anyone sits down.

💨

Dust & odor control

Water mist binds dust at the source (PM10 reduction ≥ 70%) and atomised deodorant is released above waste working areas in zoned cycles.
