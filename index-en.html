How It Works
Stable, lasting and eco-friendly

High-Pressure Misting Cooling

A high-pressure plunger pump (70~100bar) forces water through stainless-steel mist nozzles (0.15~0.3mm), atomizing into 1~15 micron ultra-fine mist that evaporates and absorbs heat — cooling, humidifying, creating landscape fog and boosting negative ions. The tiny droplets feel cool without soaking clothes or pooling on the ground.

🦟

Eco-Friendly Mosquito Barrier

Natural repellent (plant essential oil / low-tox pyrethroid formula) is diluted and atomized at high pressure into a continuous, even protective fog that attaches to mosquitoes' antennae and body for quick repellency. Green, mild-smelling and safe for people and pets.

💨

Dust Suppression & Odor Control

Fine mist collides with dust particles, aggregates and settles them — PM10 reduction over 70%. Linking to online dust monitoring enables automatic startup. Odor-control equipment atomizes natural deodorant, cutting ammonia by ≥ 85% at the source.
